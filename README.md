# SUPER STORE SALES PERFORMANCE 

## Dashboard Overview
The Super Store Sales Performance Dashboard provides an overview of business performance across four regions: East, North, South and West. It analyses sales, costs, profitability, customer purchasing behaviour, product categories, payment methods, sales channels and monthly item demand.
The project focuses on evaluating business performance, identifying regional and product-level trends, understanding customer behaviour and providing actionable recommendations to improve sales, profitability and operational efficiency.

## Business Objectives
The analysis aims to address the following business questions:
1. Measure overall sales performance during the period.
2. Identify top-performing and underperforming product categories, regions, and sales channels.
3. Compare sales contributions of major customer types.
4. Assess the effectiveness of different sales methods.
5. Provide actionable recommendations to optimize future sales campaigns and promotions.

## Key Business Insights
1.	 Regional Performance: The North recorded the highest sales and profit, while the South recorded the lowest performance.
2.	 Product Category: Clothing leads in the South and West, Furniture leads in the North, while Electronics leads in the East. This indicates differences in regional purchasing preferences.
3.	Payment Methods: Bank Transfer leads in the East and South, while Credit Card leads in the North and West.
4.	 Customer Performance: New customers generated more sales in the East and North, while Returning customers contributed more sales in the South and West.
5.	Sales Channels: Online sales lead in the North, South and West, whereas Retail sales lead in the East.
6.	 Monthly Demand: Item demand fluctuated throughout the reporting period, with noticeable declines in July, September and November.

## Business Recommendations
1.	 Improve profitability: Review operating costs, pricing and product margins to improve the relatively low overall profit margin.
2.	 Strengthen regional performance: Investigate the factors contributing to the South's lower sales and customer numbers and develop targeted improvement strategies.
3.	 Improve customer retention: Introduce loyalty programmes and targeted promotions to encourage repeat purchases, particularly in the East and North.
4.	 The business should continue strengthening its online platform while maintaining effective retail operations to maximise sales opportunities across all regions.
5.	Regularly monitor monthly sales and item demand to identify fluctuations, understand purchasing patterns and improve inventory planning.
